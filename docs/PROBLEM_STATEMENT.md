# Problem statement

## Working problem

A self-drive rental journey can involve uncertainty before, during, and after a booking: the final price may be hard to understand, a booking may change, vehicle condition may be disputed, deposit/refund status may be unclear, and complaint progress may be difficult to track. Vehicle owners also need clear booking requests and documented handovers.

These are hypotheses to investigate, not verified claims about every rental provider.

## Intended users

- Renters who want to understand the total estimated cost and terms before booking.
- Vehicle owners who need a structured way to review requests and document handovers.
- Administrators who need an auditable process for handling complaints in a demo environment.

## Proposed value

TrustCar-India will prototype transparent pricing, explicit booking states, check-in/check-out checklists, simulated deposit tracking, and traceable complaint tickets in one web experience.

## What success could look like

- Users can identify the estimated total and each fee before confirming.
- Booking state and cancellation conditions are visible.
- Pickup and return records are easy to compare.
- Users can see the status and history of a complaint.
- Core pricing and booking transitions are covered by automated tests.

## Assumptions to validate

1. Which rental pain points occur most often, and for which user groups?
2. Which pricing details do users need before confirming?
3. What evidence is fair and practical for vehicle-condition disputes?
4. What complaint response and escalation information is useful?
5. What legal, permit, insurance, and platform requirements apply to any real service?

## Out of scope for the first prototype

- Real vehicle rentals or booking fulfilment
- Real payments, deposits, or refunds
- Collection of identity documents or sensitive personal data
- Automated accusations, fraud scoring, or public ratings based on unverified complaints
- Claims that the prototype is legally compliant for commercial operations
