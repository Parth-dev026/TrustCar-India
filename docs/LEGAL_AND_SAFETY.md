# Legal and safety boundaries

This document is a project checklist, not legal advice.

## Before any real-world launch
- Obtain qualified advice on the applicable Motor Vehicles Act provisions, state transport rules, permit requirements, and the Rent-a-Cab Scheme or any applicable replacement/amendment.
- Confirm whether the proposed vehicle ownership, registration, permit, and operating model is allowed in each launch jurisdiction.
- Review insurance terms for self-drive rental/commercial use, accident handling, liability, and claims.
- Review platform, consumer-protection, tax, contract, privacy, and data-retention obligations.
- Define incident response, roadside support, dispute handling, cancellation, refund, and emergency processes.
- Do not advertise a real rental service until these issues are resolved.

## Prototype boundaries
- Use fictional listings and demo accounts.
- Do not accept real bookings or money.
- Do not collect Aadhaar, driving licence images, bank details, or other identity/payment data.
- Make simulated deposit/refund states explicit.
- Do not make automated determinations of fraud or liability.
- Do not present user-submitted complaints as verified facts.

## Research starting points
- India Code / Motor Vehicles Act, Section 75: https://indiacode.ecourtsindia.com/mv-act/section/75/
- Ministry of Road Transport and Highways / Central Motor Vehicles Rules reference: https://cgtransport.gov.in/Notification/CMVR_1989.pdf
- Parivahan permit information: https://parivahan.gov.in/content/about-permit

Verify current official law and local applicability with a qualified professional; links can change and a rule's existence alone does not establish that a specific business model is permitted.
