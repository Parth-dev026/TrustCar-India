# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment
We aim to make participation in TrustCar-India a respectful, constructive experience for everyone.

## Expected behaviour
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome different experience levels and viewpoints.
- Critique ideas, not people.
- Give feedback that helps others learn.
- Respect privacy and do not share personal information without consent.

## Unacceptable behaviour
Harassment, insults, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting
Contact the repository owner privately through GitHub if you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour. Reports will be reviewed as fairly as possible.
