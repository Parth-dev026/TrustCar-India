# Competitor and pain-point research

## Purpose
Understand which rental-journey problems matter most and whether a prototype can address them. This file is a research log, not a claim that any company is unsafe or dishonest.

## Hypotheses to test
- Users may not understand the full estimated price before booking.
- Booking changes or cancellations may create uncertainty.
- Deposit/refund timelines may be difficult to follow.
- Vehicle condition at pickup and return may be disputed.
- Support requests may lack visible status or escalation history.
- Owners may want clearer renter requests and documented handovers.

## Research method
For each source, record:
- URL and date accessed
- Source type (official policy, app review, forum post, interview, news report)
- Exact issue described, paraphrased neutrally
- Whether it is a single anecdote or a recurring pattern
- Relevant response from the provider, if available
- Confidence and unanswered questions

Do not treat individual social posts or app reviews as verified facts. Look for repeated independent reports and official policies, and distinguish allegations from established outcomes.

## Comparison dimensions
| Dimension | Questions |
|---|---|
| Pricing | Are all mandatory charges shown before confirmation? |
| Availability | Is availability confirmed before payment? How are substitutions handled? |
| Cancellation | Are rules and refund timelines easy to find? |
| Handover | Can both sides record and acknowledge vehicle condition? |
| Deposit | Is status and expected timeline visible? |
| Support | Is there a ticket number, history, and escalation route? |
| Owner experience | Are booking, damage, cancellation, and payout rules clear? |
| Legal model | Are vehicle eligibility, permits, and insurance explained? |

## Current conclusion
No pain point is considered validated yet. Use this framework to gather evidence before making strong claims or deciding the product's differentiator.
